Before you pray
  1. Make wudu if you can, and face the qibla.
  2. Begin by praising Allah and sending blessings on the Prophet (peace be upon him).
  3. Raise your hands and ask with humility and certainty.
  4. Repeat your dua, ideally three times, and also ask in your own words.
  5. End by sending blessings on the Prophet again and saying Amin.

The transliterations are a simple guide to pronunciation. Please learn the correct recitation from a qualified teacher or imam.
